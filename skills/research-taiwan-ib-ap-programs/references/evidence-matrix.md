# Evidence matrix

## IB DP

Typical required preparation evidence includes legal identity, governance, mission alignment, coordinator capacity, curriculum structure, staffing and professional development, policies, facilities/resources, budget, and an action plan. Confirm the current wording against the IB resource library and regional office. Public candidacy and authorization PDFs are preparation samples, not the submission channel.

## AP

Typical required evidence includes a designated Course Audit Administrator, teacher course forms, an accepted course-document path, administrator approval, annual renewal/attestation, and Course Ledger verification. Course-specific exceptions and special requirements must be checked on the current AP course page.

## Classification

- `required`: explicitly required by the official current process.
- `recommended`: useful local control or planning evidence, not represented as an external mandate.
- `conditional`: required only for a programme, course, delivery model, or school circumstance.
- `unknown`: needs confirmation with the official programme office.
